Loyalty Program

Earn Points – Unlock Rewards – Keep the Wellness Flowing

At Wellness Origin, your path to holistic wellness and natural beauty is something we celebrate every step of the way. Our Loyalty Program, powered by Referrizer, makes it effortless to earn rewards for the self-care you already love—turning your visits, shares, and referrals into meaningful savings on organic facials, therapeutic massages, hyperbaric oxygen therapy, body contouring, laser/IPL treatments, and more.

Our Loyalty Program is simple, automatic, and designed with you in mind—no apps to download, no cards to carry. Just relax and let the rewards accumulate.

How to Earn Points

  • 5 points — Check in for any service (automatic at your appointment)
  • 10 points — Share on Facebook (tag @wellnessorigin) — once per week
  • 10 points — Post a review (Google, Yelp, Facebook, or elsewhere)

After every visit, receive a friendly SMS text with your personal Referrizer WebApp link. Click it to:

  • View your current points balance
  • Check referral credits
  • Redeem rewards instantly
  • Share offers or your unique referral link with ease

Why Join the Wellness Origin Loyalty Program?

  • No expiration on points—redeem them anytime, as long as you stay connected with occasional visits
  • Fully automated tracking through Referrizer (secure and separate from our scheduling system)
  • Rewards the things you already do: returning for care, sharing your positive experiences, and inviting loved ones
  • Enhances your holistic journey with savings on non-toxic, organic treatments and advanced therapies tailored to your goals
