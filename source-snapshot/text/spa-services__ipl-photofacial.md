Suitable for the face, neck, chest, hands, or other areas, this therapy supports collagen production and improves skin texture, helping you achieve a smoother, more youthful appearance. ## Laser Carbon Peel Treatment

Revitalize your skin with the Laser Carbon Peel Treatment at Wellness Origin Spa, a non-invasive facial that delivers a radiant, youthful glow.

Using advanced IPL facial laser technology, this procedure harnesses the power of a carbon mask combined with precise laser energy to target acne, enlarged pores, and uneven skin tone. Ideal for those seeking a refreshed complexion without downtime, the Laser Carbon Peel Treatment is a game-changer for achieving smoother, brighter skin.

The Laser Carbon Peel Treatment works in two steps: a carbon mask is applied to penetrate deep into the pores, absorbing oil, dirt, and dead skin cells, then our IPL facial laser gently vaporizes the carbon, exfoliating the skin and stimulating collagen production. The carbon is key—it acts like a magnet, drawing out impurities and enhancing the laser’s ability to clear clogged pores and refine skin texture. This dual-action process not only cleanses deeply but also promotes a more even complexion, reduces acne, and boosts skin firmness, making it suitable for most skin types. You’ll notice a refreshed glow immediately, with optimal results after 3-6 sessions spaced 2-3 weeks apart.

The use of carbon in this treatment is what sets it apart. Carbon’s unique ability to bind with impurities ensures a thorough cleanse that goes beyond surface-level exfoliation, targeting stubborn blackheads, excess oil, and bacteria that contribute to acne. By amplifying the laser’s precision, the carbon enhances the treatment’s effectiveness, leaving your skin feeling polished and revitalized with a noticeable reduction in pore size and a smoother, more radiant finish.

At Wellness Origin Spa, our estheticians tailor each Laser Carbon Peel Treatment to your skin’s unique needs, using cutting-edge technology in our tranquil Indianapolis spa. The procedure is virtually painless, with only mild warmth felt, and requires no recovery time. It’s perfect for those with oily skin, blackheads, or dullness, though individuals with darker skin tones should consult our team first.
