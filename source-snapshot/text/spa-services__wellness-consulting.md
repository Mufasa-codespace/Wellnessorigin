Discover the ZYTO Wellness Scan

At Wellness Origin Spa, we offer the ZYTO Wellness Scan as part of our personalized wellness consulting services. This technology provides insights into your wellness needs by analyzing your body’s responses, offering a tailored approach to support your goals.

The ZYTO Wellness Scan is a simple, comfortable process. Place your hand on the ZYTO Hand Cradle for a galvanic skin response (GSR) scan, which uses subtle energetic signals to measure your body’s reactions. Most people don’t feel anything during the scan, making it a straightforward experience.

Understanding ZYTO Reports

Our ZYTO reports provide detailed wellness insights tailored to you. These reports offer a range of information—from in-depth analyses to concise summaries—designed to support your wellness journey. Each scan reflects your unique preferences and needs, giving you a clearer picture of your well-being.

ZYTO Bio Testing

Every individual’s wellness journey is unique, and the ZYTO scan helps us understand your biological preferences. This technology supports wellness consulting by providing data that can guide personalized nutrition or other wellness strategies. With a broad database and flexible bio-survey options, ZYTO helps consultants explore factors that may influence your health.

How it works

The ZYTO system facilitates an exchange of information between a computer and your body. Using galvanic skin response—a method also seen in biofeedback—it measures reactions to computer-generated virtual stimuli (energetic pulses). This process helps wellness professionals gain insights to support your health goals more effectively.

The ZYTO Elite offers access to an extensive library, including items like homeopathy, environmental factors, and more, represented as Virtual Stimulus Items (VSIs). These can be scanned manually or included in automated bio-surveys. Please note: ZYTO technology is not intended to identify, diagnose, or treat any disease or medical condition—it’s a tool for wellness exploration.
