###### Laser Hair Removal

Laser Hair Removal at Wellness Origin Spa in Carmel, IN, is a technique used to reduce unwanted facial and body hair. It involves using laser light to target hair follicles.

The process involves applying laser light in short pulses. This may cause thermal damage to the hair follicle, potentially delaying or reducing hair growth. The laser light targets melanin in the hair follicle during its active growth cycle.

Hair color and skin type can influence the effectiveness of laser hair removal. Darker hair and lighter skin may respond more effectively to treatment. Due to the hair growth cycle, a series of treatments is typically recommended.

Our laser hair removal specialist will consult with you on an individual basis, to determine the amount of treatments that will be right for you.
