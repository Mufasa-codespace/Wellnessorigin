---

Redesign the website into a premium, modern, minimal, and highly interactive digital experience.

The website should feel custom-built by a top-tier creative agency, not assembled from generic templates or AI-generated layouts.

Core philosophy: Less clutter. More interaction. Better experience.

## 1. Design Direction

- Use a clean, minimal, sophisticated design language.
- Remove unnecessary sections, repetitive content, oversized text blocks, excessive cards, and distracting elements.
- Prioritize generous whitespace, strong typography, visual hierarchy, and purposeful imagery.
- Every section must serve a clear purpose.
- Avoid generic SaaS-style designs, overused gradients, and repetitive boxed layouts.
- Make the website visually impressive without overwhelming visitors.
- Use a consistent color palette derived from the existing brand identity and supplied images.

## 2. Images & Visual Assets

IMPORTANT: Use the images and assets I have already shared.

- Prioritize my supplied images over stock photography.
- Do not replace supplied visuals with generic AI-generated images.
- Do not unnecessarily modify or recreate the original images.
- Optimize images for performance without sacrificing visual quality.
- Use imagery creatively through full-width compositions, editorial layouts, subtle parallax, and interactive galleries.
- Ensure images are appropriately cropped and remain sharp across devices.

## 3. Interactivity & Animations

Interactivity is a major priority.

Implement:

- Smooth scrolling and polished page transitions.
- Subtle scroll-triggered animations.
- Interactive hover states and microinteractions.
- Animated typography where appropriate.
- Elegant image reveals and transitions.
- Interactive service showcases.
- Responsive buttons with meaningful feedback.
- Dynamic content sections that respond naturally to user interaction.
- Subtle magnetic effects or cursor interactions where they genuinely enhance usability.
- Smooth expanding sections, accordions, and contextual reveals.

Every interaction must feel intentional, fluid, and premium.

Avoid excessive movement, distracting effects, animation overload, and unnecessary loading screens.

Support reduced-motion preferences.

## 4. Content & Layout Simplification

Audit the entire existing website.

- Remove redundant sections.
- Combine overlapping information.
- Eliminate unnecessary paragraphs.
- Replace lengthy explanations with concise, compelling messaging.
- Keep headings short and impactful.
- Reduce the number of competing calls to action.
- Create clear visual breathing room between sections.
- Make navigation effortless.

Do not remove important service information, business details, or SEO-relevant content simply for aesthetic reasons. Restructure it intelligently.

## 5. User Experience

The website should feel like an interactive experience rather than a static brochure.

- Guide users naturally toward key actions.
- Make important information accessible within minimal interactions.
- Create a strong visual journey from the hero section to the final CTA.
- Use interactive storytelling where appropriate.
- Maintain excellent readability and accessibility.
- Design primarily around real user behavior, not decorative effects.
- Ensure every interactive element has a useful purpose.

## 6. Mobile Experience

The mobile website must be equally polished.

- Fully responsive layouts.
- Touch-friendly interactions.
- Smooth and lightweight animations.
- Proper image scaling and cropping.
- Simplified mobile navigation.
- No horizontal overflow or broken elements.
- Fast loading even on slower connections.

Do not simply shrink the desktop design. Optimize the experience specifically for mobile.

## 7. Performance & Technical Quality

- Prioritize fast loading and excellent Core Web Vitals.
- Use efficient, lightweight animation techniques.
- Implement lazy loading where appropriate.
- Optimize assets and avoid unnecessary dependencies.
- Use semantic HTML and accessible components.
- Maintain proper SEO metadata, structured data, headings, and internal linking.
- Preserve important existing URLs and implement redirects where necessary.
- Ensure compatibility across modern browsers and screen sizes.

## 8. Creative Freedom

You have creative freedom to rethink the layout, typography, transitions, section arrangements, and presentation.

However, follow these boundaries:

- Do not add unnecessary sections to make the website appear larger.
- Do not use generic template designs.
- Do not sacrifice performance for animation.
- Do not replace the supplied visual assets without approval.
- Do not introduce excessive visual noise.
- Do not make every section look identical.

## 9. Expected Deliverables

1. A complete redesigned website.
2. Interactive desktop and mobile experiences.
3. Integration of all relevant supplied visual assets.
4. Simplified and refined website content.
5. Working navigation, buttons, forms, and interactions.
6. SEO-friendly and performance-optimized implementation.
7. A final quality-assurance review across devices.

## Final Standard

Minimal like Apple. Fluid like a premium creative studio. Interactive without being overwhelming.

I want visitors to feel that the website is sophisticated, responsive, memorable, and effortless to navigate.

The goal is not to add more features or more sections.

The goal is to achieve more impact with fewer elements.

Every pixel, transition, image, and interaction must justify its existence.
