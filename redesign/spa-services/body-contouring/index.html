Body Contouring Treatment

This Service Incorporates the following modalities:

  • Lipo Cavitation (Ultrasonic Therapy): This modality, when included in our combined service, utilizes low-frequency sound waves applied to targeted areas. The process involves using ultrasonic energy to create vibrations within the targeted areas.
  • Radio Frequency Contouring: This modality, when included in our combined service, uses radio-frequency energy applied to the skin. The process involves using controlled heat to target skin layers.
  • Cupping Treatment: This modality, when included in our combined service, involves placing cups on the skin to create suction. The process can create a localized change in pressure.
  • Red Light Therapy: This modality, when included in our combined service, uses specific wavelengths of red light applied to the skin. The process involves exposing the skin to specific light wavelengths.

These treatments are intended to complement a holistic wellness approach and a healthy lifestyle. We recommend consulting with our experienced practitioners to discuss your individual needs and to determine if our combined body contouring service is suitable for you.

Important Considerations:

Body contouring treatments are not recommended for individuals who are pregnant or breastfeeding, have implanted devices (e.g., pacemakers, insulin pumps), or have a seizure disorder. Please disclose any medical conditions during your consultation.
