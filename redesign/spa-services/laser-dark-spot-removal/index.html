Transform Your Skin with Laser Dark Spot Removal

Transform Your Skin with Laser Dark Spot Removal Dark spots, or hyperpigmentation, caused by sun exposure, aging, hormones, or acne scars can affect your confidence. Laser dark spot removal at Wellness Origin Spa offers a fast, effective solution to achieve a brighter, more even complexion.

Why Choose Laser Treatment?

  • Targeted Precision : Lasers focus on pigment cells, leaving surrounding skin untouched.
  • Quick Results : Visible improvements often seen in just a few sessions.
  • Long-Lasting Effects : With proper sun protection, results can last.
  • Minimal Downtime : Resume daily activities quickly with modern laser technology.
  • Added Benefits : Stimulates collagen for smoother skin and improves overall tone.

How It Works Lasers break down melanin clusters in dark spots using specific light wavelengths. Your body naturally clears these fragments, fading spots over time. Treatment is tailored to your skin type and hyperpigmentation severity.

Preparation & Aftercare

  • Before : Avoid sun exposure for 2-4 weeks, use SPF 30+ daily, and inform us of medications.
  • After : Moisturize, apply SPF 30+, and avoid irritants like harsh products or hot showers.

Treatment Plans Multiple sessions, spaced weeks apart, are typically needed for optimal results. Our practitioner customizes each session for your comfort and goals.
