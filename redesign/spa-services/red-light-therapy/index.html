Red Light Therapy

Red Light Therapy Full Body (30 min)

Lie-down red light therapy at Wellness Origin Spa in Carmel, Indiana. In this 30-minute session you rest on the table while red and near-infrared light treat the front and back of the body at the same time . No standing. No flipping over halfway through.

The light we use This session uses a full-body blanket with:

  • Visible red light at 660 nm
  • Near-infrared light at 850 nm (not visible)
  • Dual-wavelength LEDs designed for surface and deeper tissue in one wrap
  • Manufacturer-stated irradiance over 170 mW/cm² at the surface

These are the two wavelengths most often studied for recovery and skin-support protocols.

What to Expect Change into underwear or a swimsuit. We provide a table barrier and protective goggles. The blanket wraps so both sides receive light in one visit. Sessions feel comfortable, with a gentle warmth and no downtime. Most clients use this after a massage, after a workout, or as a standalone recovery visit. For best results, plan a short series rather than a single session.

Please note: Tell us if you are pregnant, using photosensitizing medication, or have a condition that makes you sensitive to light. This is a wellness service, not a medical treatment.

What to wear / bring Underwear or a swimsuit. Remove lotions and oils from the treatment area. Goggles are provided.

Red Light Therapy Full Body + Face (30 min)

Full-body red light therapy plus a face mask in one 30-minute visit at Wellness Origin Spa in Carmel. You lie down while the blanket treats the front and back of the body at the same time , and a silicone mask delivers red and near-infrared light to the face. Recovery and a brighter-looking complexion in the same appointment — no standing and no turning over.

What to Expect The body blanket and face mask run together so you stay on the table for 30 minutes. Red light supports the look of the skin at the surface. Near-infrared light is used for deeper comfort and recovery. Goggles are provided. There is no downtime. This pairs well after a facial or as its own glow-and-reset visit. A series of sessions is recommended for the most consistent results.

Please note: Please tell us if you are pregnant, using photosensitizing medication, or have a condition that makes you sensitive to light. This is a wellness service, not a medical treatment.

Pricing

  • Single session — $74
  • 3 Session Package — $198 (Savings $24)
  • 6 Session Package — $375 (Savings $69)

What to wear / bring Underwear or a swimsuit. Come with a clean face (no heavy makeup or thick cream). Goggles are provided.
